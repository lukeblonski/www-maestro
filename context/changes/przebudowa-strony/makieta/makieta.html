Your partner

This is not another code monkey. This is your partner in building meaningful things.

Maestro offloads you from everything AI does better, so you can focus on what only humans do best.

  • You decide what gets built.

    Maestro helps you describe how your idea should work, then helps you choose the technology, the infrastructure and everything else it takes.

  • Your idea, translated for the AI agents that build it.

    You and Maestro agree on what you want to build. Maestro turns that into precise work for AI agents and keeps them on track until it is done.

  • Autonomy whenever it makes sense.

    Maestro gathers everything it needs for the build up front, so it involves you as little as possible once the work starts. It can even configure the external services your product needs, once you give it access.

  • Plain language, always.

    Maestro talks to you in everyday words, so you understand everything and know exactly what is happening.

  • It remembers.

    Maestro remembers your decisions and the reasons behind them for as long as you want. Its entire memory is stored on your server.

You decide

Maestro does the work. You make the calls.

  • Nothing important without your approval.

    Installations, spending, sharing data, publishing, new accounts and anything that cannot be undone wait for your yes.

  • Every question comes with a recommendation.

    Maestro explains what you are agreeing to in plain language and tells you which answer it recommends and why.

  • Questions come to your phone.

    When Maestro needs your decision, you get a notification in the Claude app, on your computer or phone.

From build to deploy

Building is half the job. Maestro puts your product in front of your users.

  • See it working first.

    As each part is finished, you can open a private preview and try your product before anyone else does.

  • Deployed for you.

    Maestro not only helps you build your product. It deploys it, so your users can reach it.

  • Released when you say so.

    Nothing goes public without your approval.

FAQ

A few things you might be wondering.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. To set up Maestro, paste an installation command into your server provider’s browser terminal and sign in to your own accounts. The installer provides checks and guidance.

What will it cost?

You pay for your own server and Claude subscription directly with their providers. Your application may need additional paid services, which require your approval and an agreed limit. Your Claude plan’s usage limits apply.

When can I start?

Join the waiting list and we’ll email you as soon as the first release is ready.

Does Maestro do anything without asking me?

No. Installations, spending, sharing data, publishing, new accounts and anything that cannot be undone need your approval first. Maestro explains what you are agreeing to.

Where are my data, code and project history?

Your application data and Maestro’s memory live on your server. Your code and project history live in your own private repository. If a feature needs to send data to an AI or other external service, Maestro asks for your approval and explains what will be shared.

What happens if I stop using Maestro?

Your code, application data, memory and decision history remain yours. Your server and other services are in your accounts, so you can keep using or developing your application. Their ongoing charges remain your responsibility.

Will it tell me whether my idea is worth building?

The first release includes a focused conversation and questions that challenge your assumptions. Full Discovery, with deeper research, assumption tests and a reasoned build, change or stop recommendation, is planned for a later release.

Does the first release maintain my application automatically?

Not yet. Looking after your product and helping it grow are coming soon. The first release focuses on taking your idea through to a working application.
